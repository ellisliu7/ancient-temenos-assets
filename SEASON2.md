## Sacred Transmutation (the offering rite)

When a collector gathers a certain combination, they may offer keys back to the temple.

Not burning. Not destroying. **Surrendering** — and receiving something unknown in return.

Possible returns (undecided, deliberately mysterious):

- A rarer, evolving sigil
- A hidden chamber unlock
- Founder relic
- Living key
- Secret oracle
- Ancient artifact
- Animated evolving piece
- Access to hidden writings
- A personal blessing / message

Principles:

- The reveal moment is the heart of it — anticipation, then transformation
- The seeker should never know exactly what returns
- Secret chamber unlocks and founder relics reward the earliest initiates
