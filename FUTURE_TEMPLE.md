> One of four living documents. This is the vault where every dream is kept safe — so none are lost, and none distract from today's work.

## The rule of this vault

> **Presence here is preservation, not permission.**

An idea living in Future Temple is *safe* — it will not be forgotten. But it is not *approved*, *scheduled*, or *next*. An idea only leaves this vault when three things are true: it passes the **Temple Compass**, its horizon has arrived, and it is the current **One Thing's** turn. Until then, it rests here, and we return to today's stone.

Everything below is arranged by category, with: its **essence**, the **emotional job** it does for the visitor, its **earliest horizon** (Year 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 10 — deliberately conservative), its **preconditions**, and a **guardian flag** where the dilution risk is high.

## Digital core

### Healing Chambers (Persephone · Psyche · beyond)
- **Essence:** The world grows one deity at a time. Persephone (descent, shadow, rebirth) is prototyped and next as a *production* item; Psyche (inner child, wonder, play) follows; further chambers only if the mythology genuinely calls for them.
- **Emotional job:** Each chamber lets a returning visitor go somewhere new inside themselves. A larger world = a deeper reason to return.
- **Horizon (Production):** Persephone — Year 1 (once her assets are beautiful and she's chosen as a committed build). Psyche — Year 1–2. Further chambers — Year 3+.
- **Preconditions:** The prior chamber must be complete and beloved; the new chamber's artwork must exist to standard before any wiring. *Chambers exist to justify the council — never build a chamber to fill a menu.*
- **Guardian flag:** Do not integrate an under-finished chamber to hit a date. A half-finished sacred room is worse than a "Sealed" one.
- **Note — active Creative R&D (Jul 2026):** El is currently prototyping with the Healing Chambers *concept* as playful, exploratory work — using the existing world to learn about atmosphere, ritual, and long-form presence. This is explicitly **not** a commitment to build Persephone/Psyche next, and its findings do not automatically become roadmap items. See Temple Compass → Production vs. Creative R&D. If this exploration surfaces something the temple should commit to, it graduates through the Compass like any other idea.

### The Sacred Calendar (a temple that ages)
- **Essence:** The temple changes with real time — solstices, moon phases, seasons — so returning feels like visiting a living place with its own liturgy.
- **Emotional job:** Turns visitors into *pilgrims* who return on specific days. The deepest engagement in this whole landscape comes from worlds that move without you.
- **Horizon:** Year 2 (modest code, large payoff — one of the highest-return ideas relative to cost).
- **Preconditions:** The core experience is proven and returned-to; there's a reason to come back on a *particular* day.

### Collective Memory room
- **Essence:** A room built from visitors' own consented, anonymized reflections — the community co-authoring a sacred text.
- **Emotional job:** The visitor sees they are part of something larger than themselves; utterly un-clonable because it is *this* community's real words.
- **Horizon:** Year 2–3. Build the **pipe** (Grimoire fragment accumulation) now; build the **room** only when there's real data.
- **Guardian flag:** An empty Collective Memory room is hollow and would cheapen the idea. Patience is the whole design.

## Content & atmosphere

### YouTube (atmosphere + doorway, not a content mill)
- **Essence:** Long-form ambient renders of the chambers — sacred but non-religious, built on El's *own* art and (eventually) *owned* sound. Each video is a doorway *back* to the temple.
- **Emotional job:** Meets the enormous ambient audience in the place they already rest, and leads the ones who feel something home.
- **Horizon:** One render — end of Year 1 (Roadmap Movement III). A real channel — Year 1–2, only after one render proves the model.
- **Preconditions:** The room is worth entering (Stages 0–2 true); owned or licensed sound; owned art (never borrowed IP — that's the fatal shortcut that kills fantasy-ambience channels).
- **Guardian flag:** Do not become wallpaper. The point is depth and return, not view-count. One perfect artifact beats a schedule of forgettable ones.

### Original Sound (owned sonic identity → later, a label)
- **Essence:** An original Ancient Temenos sound — owned, unclonable — releasable on Spotify/Apple/YouTube as its own doorway, and eventually the seed of a label.
- **Emotional job:** Sound is the fastest route to the body and to awe; it becomes both a second content stream and a moat (the Lofi Records lesson).
- **Horizon:** Sonic *identity* — Year 1–2. A *label* — Year 3+.
- **Preconditions:** A composer/collaborator aligned with the voice; start with identity, never enterprise.
- **Guardian flag:** Sound design has been scoped and reverted before. Only commit when the result would meet the standard — no stock cues, no "meditation-app" sonic clichés.
- **Status (5 Jul 2026):** Long-form Ancient Temenos sound world in active **Creative R&D** — exploration, not commitment (per Temple Compass, Production vs. Creative R&D). Distinct from, and not to be conflated with, interaction-consequence sound — an architectural question tracked in CURRENT_STATE open signals (Oliver, n=1; the rite's single bell is its first test). Mixing note from the field: the ambient bed must survive real rooms — a tester's drone vanished under an air conditioner. Dynamics, not more material.

### The Atelier (El's hand as sacred content)
- **Essence:** A slow, silent, beautiful stream/series of El *creating* the artwork — the atelier itself as content.
- **Emotional job:** The ultimate "human, not AI" signal (decisive as AI floods the space); un-fakeable authorship; process as sacred. teamLab and Meow Wolf *cannot* do this — they're faceless. El's hand is precisely what they lack.
- **Horizon:** Year 1–2 (a natural companion to the altar-layer timelapse).
- **Preconditions:** Willingness to be visible as the maker; the atelier framed as sacred, not "behind-the-scenes."
- **Status (5 Jul 2026):** Elevated from idea to **validated architectural hypothesis**. Two-way bridge designed (daylight door ↔ temple: art discovery → Atelier → "this artwork has a world?" → temple; and temple → chamber → "this world began with a real artwork?" → physical work). Three-page shell built (Atelier landing / Venus / Ganymede & the Eagle). Venus edition concept specified: faithful reproduction on etching paper, gold acrylic repainted by El's own hand, same material logic as the original, signed and numbered, small honest edition. **Deliberately not promoted above the Rite of Departure.** Unlock condition: the rite has shipped and the exit pattern is confirmed healed. Guardian flag: the Atelier must remain a door, never a store.

## Objects people carry home

### Ritual Journals
- **Essence:** The Grimoire made physical — a printed, made-to-order sacred journal that carries the temple's ritual into the visitor's daily life.
- **Emotional job:** Extends the relationship off-screen; something to hold in hard moments.
- **Horizon:** Year 1 (low difficulty, high margin, high emotional resonance — a strong first physical artifact).
- **Preconditions:** People already love the digital experience; production/fulfillment partner sourced.

### Prints
- **Essence:** El's artwork as museum-grade physical prints.
- **Emotional job:** The visitor puts a piece of the temple on their wall — the world enters their home.
- **Horizon:** Year 1 (high margin, low risk).
- **Preconditions:** Print partner meeting the quality bar; the art is the hero, framing is restrained.

### Portal Frames
- **Essence:** Bespoke framed artworks that act as **architectural thresholds** into the Ancient Temenos world — custom-built, sculptural, and emotionally significant rather than conventional picture frames. Not a print-in-a-frame; the frame itself is part of the sacred object, designed as a physical portal, not packaging.
- **Emotional job:** A visitor keeps an actual architectural fragment of the temple in their own space — the threshold experience made permanent and physical, not a souvenir.
- **Horizon:** Year 2–3.
- **Preconditions:** Prints established (as a simpler, earlier expression of "art on the wall"); a sculptor/fabricator relationship capable of the craft standard; the design language for the frame-as-architecture worked out on paper before any production commitment.
- **Guardian flag:** This sits close to Sculptures in ambition — treat it with the same care and the same "never ship before beautiful" standard. Do not let it collapse into a conventional framed-print product; the architectural, sculptural quality is the entire point.

### Books (the mythology / art tome)
- **Essence:** The canonical Ancient Temenos book — the art and the mythology, fixed in a form that outlives any platform.
- **Emotional job:** Gives the world permanence and gravity; becomes the artifact people gift and museums stock.
- **Horizon:** Year 2–3.
- **Preconditions:** The mythology is mature; enough artwork exists; a publishing/production path worthy of it.

### Collectibles (reflection-gated — the deliberate anti-NFT)
- **Essence:** El's artwork collectible *only after* answering a reflection question (the Venus question is locked). Positioned explicitly as the opposite of speculative crypto collecting — "art you earn by looking inward, not by paying to flip."
- **Emotional job:** Makes acquisition an act of self-reflection; a genuinely new model of artist patronage.
- **Horizon:** Year 2–3.
- **Preconditions:** Stage 2 true; the anti-NFT framing designed so it never reads as crypto.
- **Guardian flag:** The crypto aesthetic is on the Bible's forbidden list. The reflection gate is the whole defense — if it ever feels like speculation, stop.

## Physical sanctuaries & experiences

### Installations (small-footprint, sanctuary scale)
- **Essence:** A single, exquisite immersive room — the teamLab/Meow Wolf lesson at *sanctuary scale, not spectacle scale.*
- **Emotional job:** Awe made bodily and shared, without losing intimacy.
- **Horizon:** Year 3–5.
- **Preconditions:** Digital body complete; start as a pop-up or a room *inside* an existing space, never a ground-up build.

### The Parasitic Sanctuary (a room inside places people already go)
- **Essence:** One perfect Ancient Temenos room installed inside a boutique hotel, a wellness resort (Bali / Chiang Mai), a museum contemplation space, an airport meditation room. (Calm-into-Hilton proves hospitality wants exactly this.)
- **Emotional job:** Meets people where they already come to feel something; serves the SE Asia mission *and* self-funds the installation.
- **Horizon:** Year 3–5 (the low-capital path into the physical world).
- **Preconditions:** An aligned partner space; the room meets the digital standard.

### Physical Exhibitions
- **Essence:** A larger curated Ancient Temenos exhibition.
- **Emotional job:** The world at full scale, for many, for a season.
- **Horizon:** Year 5+.
- **Preconditions:** Brand genuinely iconic; institutional or partner backing (do **not** self-finance a nine-figure build — Meow Wolf raised $192M; that is not the path here).

### Sculptures
- **Essence:** The archetypes made physical.
- **Horizon:** Year 3–5.
- **Preconditions:** Demand from people who already love the world; the physical era has begun.

### Retreats
- **Essence:** Ancient Temenos as a physical sanctuary experience, ideally in SE Asia — serving the $1T+ wellness-tourism market *and* the founding mission.
- **Emotional job:** The deepest, most embodied form of the temple.
- **Horizon:** Year 4–5.
- **Preconditions:** Iconic brand; a concrete home; operational capacity (this is the heaviest thing on the list).
- **Guardian flag:** Avoid clinical/therapeutic claims (regulatory risk) — Ancient Temenos meets people at *thresholds*, it does not treat.

### Museums (hosting / acquisition)
- **Essence:** Ancient Temenos positioned not only as a product but as a *work* institutions host or acquire.
- **Emotional job:** Legitimacy and legacy; opens grants, residencies, non-dilutive funding.
- **Horizon:** Year 5–10.
- **Preconditions:** A body of work of undeniable coherence and craft; "legendary status is a consequence of restraint and time."

## Relationship & stewardship

### Patron Experiences
- **Essence:** A *small* circle of deep patrons who fund the mission — high-touch, high-margin, low-volume — rather than mass low-value transactions.
- **Emotional job:** The most aligned people become custodians of the temple; their support directly serves "when the temple earns, women benefit."
- **Horizon:** Year 2–3.
- **Preconditions:** A beloved world; a patron offering that is genuinely sacred (access, presence, artifacts), not a "tier."

### Mobile App
- **Essence:** The temple in your pocket — a daily ritual.
- **Horizon:** Year 3+.
- **Preconditions:** The web temple is beloved and returned-to; a daily use case the web can't serve. (See The One Thing → Not Yet: the app market is *contracting*; enter from strength.)

### The Endowment Inversion (the temple as a giving engine)
- **Essence:** Structure Ancient Temenos so that, as it grows, a *permanent* portion of revenue endows women's education and empowerment in Asia and SE Asia.
- **Emotional job / strategic value:** The mission becomes the brand's deepest moat and most compelling patron story (Meow Wolf's B-Corp + foundation model proves a soul-level mission attracts capital and press, not repels them).
- **Horizon:** The *structure* — early (Year 1–2, while it's easy). The *scale* — as revenue grows.
- **Preconditions:** None to begin thinking; formalize once there's revenue to give from.

## How to use this document
- The instant an exciting idea arrives, **write it here** — that's how you free yourself to keep working on today's One Thing.
- Nothing moves from here into the Roadmap without passing the **Temple Compass** *and* arriving at its horizon *and* being the **One Thing's** turn.
- Revisit the vault a few times a year (e.g. at each Movement's close). Some ideas will have ripened; some will have quietly revealed themselves as the "evil twin" of something sacred, and can be released. Both are healthy.
