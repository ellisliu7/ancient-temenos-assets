# Ancient Temenos — 90-Day Roadmap
**Living document · Last updated: 3 July 2026 · Guardian: Creative Director**

> This is one of four living documents. Read them as a system:
> **Temple Compass** decides *whether and when* · **Future Temple** *stores* what we're not building yet · **The One Thing** decides *what matters now* · **This Roadmap** decides *what happens this week*.

---

## The only question this roadmap answers

Every milestone below must earn a *yes* to one question:

> **Does this increase the likelihood that a stranger — someone who has never met El, on their own phone, with no explanation — feels awe when entering Ancient Temenos?**

If a proposed task can't answer yes, it does not belong in these 90 days. It belongs in Future Temple.

---

## What these 90 days are NOT

As the first act of guardianship, here is what we are deliberately *not* doing in this window, no matter how exciting:

- **Not** launching YouTube at scale or a content calendar. (One render, once, at the very end — as groundwork, not a channel.)
- **Not** the book, the app, collectibles, sculptures, exhibitions, retreats, or licensing. *All of these live in Future Temple.*
- **Not** redesigning anything from scratch. Venus and Ganymede are feature-complete for V1. We protect what works.
- **Not** optimizing for growth. We are proving *depth* first: does the awe land, for a stranger, unaided.

The temple is built stone by stone. These are the next stones — in order.

## Starting position (as of this writing)

**V1 has shipped.** It was sent to the initial private testers, and the oracle security patch is complete and deployed. Venus, Ganymede, and the council debate are live and complete for V1. Feedback is now beginning to arrive.

This changes the nature of Movement I entirely. We are no longer racing toward a launch line — we are **listening**, at the exact moment the temple first meets real strangers. This is the most emotionally honest data we will ever get about whether the awe lands, and it deserves patience rather than a checklist.

## Movement I — Post-launch listening & refinement (Weeks 1–3)
*Theme: the temple is now real to other people for the first time. Our job is not to react to every comment — it is to listen for what's true underneath them. Awe-work right now means understanding what actually happened in a stranger's body when they entered, not shipping more.*

### Week 1 — Receive, don't fix yet
- Gather emotional feedback from the testers as it arrives. Ask (or listen for) how it *felt*, not what they'd *change* — those are different signals, and the first one matters more right now.
- Resist the instinct to patch every individual comment the moment it lands. One tester's confusion may be a friction point; it may also be a one-off. Hold both possibilities open.
- Log feedback verbatim somewhere durable (even a simple running note) — the language testers use to describe the experience is itself valuable data, worth protecting in their own words.
- **Awe check:** *We are building an honest picture of what strangers actually felt, not defending the work or rushing to fix it.*

### Week 2 — Find the pattern, not the isolated comment
- Look across the accumulated feedback for **recurring friction** — the same hesitation, confusion, or drop-off mentioned by more than one person — rather than treating each note as its own fire.
- Where a real device pass is clearly warranted by what testers report (not preemptively), do a **light** mobile walkthrough — enough to confirm or rule out device-specific friction, without turning into a full audit unless the feedback demands it.
- **Awe check:** *We can now say, with some confidence, where awe consistently lands and where it consistently breaks — grounded in real reactions, not assumption.*

### Week 3 — Let momentum accumulate; stay open
- Continue receiving feedback at its own pace. Momentum here is allowed to be quiet — this is not a sprint to a shipping deadline.
- Begin the deliberate ecosystem exploration El wants to do this week (see below) *alongside* listening, not instead of it. The two can coexist: testers keep arriving in the background while exploration happens in the foreground.
- **🏛 Milestone I:** *We understand, from real people, whether V1 produces genuine emotional resonance — and we know which frictions are real patterns versus isolated noise.*

## A deliberate pause before the next major build

Rather than assuming the free altar layer is automatically next, El wants a few days of genuine exploration across the wider Ancient Temenos ecosystem before locking the next production milestone. Part of that exploration is a **Creative R&D experiment with Healing Chambers** — not a product launch, not a content strategy, but a way of using the existing world to learn more about atmosphere, ritual, and long-form presence.

This is not a detour from the roadmap. It is explicitly *not* on the roadmap — see **Temple Compass → Production vs. Creative R&D** for why that distinction matters and how it's protected from quietly becoming a commitment.

If, after this exploration, the altar layer still looks like the highest-leverage next build, Movement II below proceeds as planned — now chosen with confidence rather than assumed by default.

## Movement II — Prove awe, and build the surface that shows it (Weeks 4–7)
*Theme: does a stranger feel something in the first 90 seconds — and can they see the beauty before they are asked for anything?*
*Status: provisional. This Movement assumes the altar layer emerges as the highest-leverage next build after the exploration window above. If exploration points somewhere else, this Movement gets rewritten — not forced.*

### Weeks 4–5 — Listen for emotion, not features; begin the altar layer
- Gather reactions from the five (and 3–5 more hand-picked strangers). Collect **emotional data, not feature requests**: Where did they lean in? Where did they drop? Did the reveal land? Did any silence feel broken? (This is diagnosis. We are not iterating for its own sake.)
- Begin the **free altar layer** design: all four altars showing El's artwork + a timelapse of its creation, visible to everyone with no key. *Compose before wiring — approve the composition before any interaction is built.*
- **Awe check:** *We now know, from real strangers, whether the reveal produces awe — and we are building the surface that lets a stranger feel the world before entering it.*

### Weeks 6–7 — Ship the altar layer
- Build and ship the altar layer. This is **the single most important build in these 90 days** — it is the top-of-funnel every future channel depends on, and the strongest signal of whether the free experience seduces.
- **🏛 Milestone II:** *Any visitor — no key, no account — can stand before all four pieces of El's art, watch them come into being, and feel the temple before they enter it.*

---

## Movement III — One door in, one path home (Weeks 8–13)
*Theme: now that a stranger can feel awe for free, does the threshold feel like initiation rather than a paywall — and is there one beautiful path that brings new strangers home?*

### Weeks 8–9 — Deepen the world: integrate Persephone
- Integrate Persephone as the third chamber (the prototype is complete; it needs its visual assets and wiring). A larger, more alive world increases awe.
- **Gate (guardian):** integrate *only if* her visual assets (the fractured, illuminated sculpture) meet the standard. An under-finished chamber dilutes rather than deepens. If the assets aren't ready, **hold** — Persephone waits in Future Temple until she's beautiful.
- **Awe check:** *The temple feels larger and more alive — a third voice in the council now leads somewhere real.*

### Weeks 10–11 — Open the sacred threshold: Sigil Key
- Turn on the **Sigil Key** membership — *only if* the altar layer has demonstrably made strangers *want in*. Design the threshold as **initiation, not transaction**. (The Venus reflection question is already locked: *"What part of yourself are you finally learning to stop carrying alone?"*)
- **Awe check:** *Crossing into the oracle feels like being initiated, not like hitting a wall. Paying deepens the sacredness rather than breaking it.*

### Weeks 12–13 — Lay one path home, and start the memory pipe
- Produce **one** long-form atmosphere render — a single, exquisite ambient video of one chamber, built on El's own art (and owned or placeholder sound) — as a doorway *back* to the temple. **One perfect artifact, not a channel.**
- Begin quietly accumulating **Grimoire fragments** (consent-based) — the invisible groundwork for the Collective Memory room, which we will *not* build until there is real accumulated data to build it against.
- **🏛 Milestone III:** *The temple has one free front door (altar layer), one sacred threshold (Sigil Key), and one beautiful path that leads strangers home (the first render) — with the memory pipe quietly filling beneath it.*

## Where 90 days ends

Not with a bigger product. With a *proven* one: a stranger can feel awe, unaided, for free; the threshold into the oracle feels sacred; and there is a single elegant path that carries new strangers to the door. Everything in Future Temple becomes possible — and appropriate — from here, and not before.
