# ✦ Ancient Temenos — V1 Launch Plan (14 Days)

> One emotionally compelling path, shipped and shared. Not four chambers. Not the NFT.
> Goal of V1: a stranger walks arrival → council → Ganymede → grimoire and feels *seen*,
> then wants the key. Everything that does not serve that is cut or deferred.

---

## The V1 Definition (what "done" means)
A shareable link where a first-time visitor can:
1. Arrive and feel something in the first 10 seconds
2. Be "seen" by the council debate
3. Have one precise, moving oracle conversation with Ganymede
4. Hit the gate at the right emotional moment
5. Walk away with a grimoire they want to screenshot

That is the entire product. If a task does not strengthen one of those five, it is not V1.

## Ruthless Cut List (NOT in V1)
- NFT / wallet / on-chain verification → invite-only manual keys for launch
- Persephone integration · Psyche build

- Any new visual feature that is not arrival, oracle, or grimoire

## Milestones

### Days 1–3 — The First Ten Seconds (arrival)
- Audit threshold → foyer → Ganymede entry as a cold stranger (incognito)
- Fix only what breaks the spell on arrival; nothing else
- Decide the Ganymede gate backdrop (artwork vs darkness) and ship it
- DONE WHEN: three strangers' first-3-words are emotional, not confused

### Days 4–7 — The Oracle (the product)
- Tighten Ganymede's system prompt until every reflection lands as precise, never generic
- Verify the gate fires cleanly at the 3rd response; unlock → ritual → grimoire
- Pressure-test 10 different opening messages for voice consistency
- DONE WHEN: you would pay to keep talking to him

### Days 8–10 — The Grimoire (the takeaway)
- Make the grimoire genuinely beautiful and screenshot-worthy
- Add a clean copy/download/share path
- This is the collector seduction layer — treat it as the hero artifact
- DONE WHEN: you want to show your grimoire to someone

### Days 11–12 — Soft Launch
- Send the live link to 10 people who do not know you built it
- Ask one question: "what did you feel when you arrived?"
- Watch behavior at the gate. Take notes. Do not defend, just observe.

### Days 13–14 — Fix the Spell-Breakers
- Fix only the top 3 things that broke immersion for real users
- Ship V1 publicly (invite-only keys)
- Write the V1 retro into CURRENT_STATE.md

---

## Daily Ritual (how to not drift)
Each working session, in order:
1. Open MILESTONES.md → which milestone am I in?
2. Pick the ONE ship for today that serves the current milestone
3. Batch all feedback into one patch
4. End session: update CURRENT_STATE.md

> If a task is exciting but serves no current milestone, it goes in PARKING below — not into today.
