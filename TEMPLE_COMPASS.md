# Ancient Temenos — Temple Compass
**Living document · Last updated: 3 July 2026 · Guardian: Creative Director**

> One of four living documents. This is the instrument we run *every* idea through before building it. Its purpose is not to say yes. Its purpose is to **protect restraint** — to make sure Ancient Temenos is built stone by stone like a cathedral, not feature by feature like a startup.

---

## The ritual: how the Compass is used

Before anything is built, an idea passes through three gates in order. If it fails an earlier gate, the later ones don't matter.

1. **The Sacred Veto.** Does the idea violate any of the seven sacred conditions? If yes → **it is a no**, regardless of how good it looks. No score can override the veto.
2. **The Score.** How worthy is the idea, weighted toward soul over money? This tells us *how good* it is.
3. **The Timing.** Given its preconditions and difficulty, which year does it belong to — and is it the current **One Thing's** turn? A worthy idea in the wrong year still waits in **Future Temple**.

An idea only reaches the **90-Day Roadmap** when it clears the veto, scores well, *and* its year has arrived.

---

## Before the gates: Production vs. Creative R&D

The Compass exists to protect focus — but focus without curiosity would eventually starve the temple of new life. So before anything else, ask which mode a piece of work is in:

**Production** — work intended to ship, become part of the live temple, or move onto the Roadmap. This is what the three gates below govern. Production follows the Roadmap; it is scored, timed, and committed.

**Creative R&D** — intentionally playful, exploratory work whose purpose is to help El (and the studio) *discover* future directions — atmosphere, ritual, presence, feeling — without changing current priorities or becoming a commitment. Creative R&D:
- does **not** need to pass the Sacred Veto, be scored, or be timed, because it is not asking permission to exist in the temple — it's asking permission to exist in the studio's imagination, which is always granted.
- does **not** automatically graduate onto the Roadmap or into Production, no matter how good it turns out. If it's exciting enough to want to build for real, it *then* enters at Gate 1 like any other idea — the exploration doesn't skip the line, it just means the idea arrives at the gate already partly understood.
- **does** get logged in Future Temple if it produces an idea worth remembering, exactly like any other dream.
- is protected *by* the Founder Energy veto, not exempt from its spirit: play, curiosity, and exploration are not indulgences competing with the real work. They are part of what keeps the studio — and the artist — creatively alive over ten years, not just the next ninety days.

The distinction matters because a studio with no room to explore eventually calcifies, and a studio that treats every exploration as a pending commitment eventually burns out. Both failure modes are avoided by naming the mode you're in *before* you start.

## Gate 1 — The Sacred Veto

*Any single one of these makes the answer no. These encode the Bible's "avoid" list and El's stated boundaries. They are not negotiable, and no revenue figure buys an exception.*

1. **Forbidden aesthetic.** It requires or drifts toward SaaS UI, crypto aesthetics, generic fantasy, over-rendered concept art, wellness-brand gloss, or dark horror.
2. **Artwork demoted.** It removes, deprioritizes, or treats El's artwork as decoration. (The artwork is the soul; it is always present, always sacred, revealed at the emotional peak.)
3. **Clutter or noise.** It adds clutter, or breaks the silence and restraint. (Ancient Temenos whispers; it never shouts. Silence is structural, not a loading state.)
4. **No awe, no return.** It cannot answer *yes* to: "Does this help a stranger feel awe, or help someone return?"
5. **Frame-breaking monetization.** It optimizes short-term growth at the cost of the sacred frame — ads, aggressive paywalls, engagement-bait, influencer/manifestation positioning.
6. **Borrowed IP.** It depends on someone else's myth, music, or characters. (This is the shortcut that kills fantasy-ambience channels. We never take it.)
7. **Shipped before beautiful.** It would go live under-finished to hit a date. (A half-finished sacred thing is worse than a "Sealed" one.)
8. **Founder Energy.** *Does this decision preserve El's ability to keep creating original artwork and meaningful places?* If it would exhaust, distract, or hollow out the person the entire studio depends on — if not, it should be delayed, redesigned, or rejected.

> If you're unsure whether something trips a veto, that hesitation is usually the answer. When in doubt: choose restraint over spectacle.

### On Founder Energy specifically

This veto is different in kind from the others — it isn't about the *artifact*, it's about the *maker*. The studio does not depend on shipping the most features. It depends on El remaining able, for another decade, to keep making original work with the depth and craft that got Ancient Temenos here at all. A decision can pass every other gate — beautiful, aligned, defensible, timeless — and still be wrong *right now* if it would burn out the one irreplaceable resource the whole cathedral is built from.

In practice, this means: pacing that respects real creative and emotional bandwidth, not just calendar availability; permission to slow down or pause without that reading as failure; and treating exploration and rest as legitimate uses of time, not deviations from "real work." Protecting the artist *is* protecting the product — there is no version of this studio that survives El's creative energy running out.

## Gate 2 — The Score

Rate each axis **1–5** using the anchors below.

### The Cathedral axes — weighted ×3 (these decide almost everything)

**Emotional impact** — how deeply does it move a real human?
`1` clever but cold · `3` pleasant · `5` produces awe, tears, or genuine remembering.

**Timelessness** — will it still feel true in a decade?
`1` trend-chasing, dates fast · `3` durable · `5` could sit in the temple in ten years unchanged.

**Alignment with Ancient Temenos** — is it unmistakably *this* world?
`1` could belong to any wellness/art brand · `3` fits · `5` could only exist inside Ancient Temenos.

### The Endurance axes — weighted ×2

**Defensibility** — how hard is it to copy or out-spend?
`1` anyone could replicate it tomorrow · `3` some moat · `5` rooted in owned art / owned experience / this community's own words — effectively uncopyable.

**Brand value** — does it make Ancient Temenos more itself, more revered?
`1` neutral or diluting · `3` supportive · `5` deepens the myth and the reverence.

### The Fuel axis — weighted ×1 (deliberately the lowest weight)

**Revenue potential** — how much can it sustainably earn?
`1` negligible · `3` meaningful · `5` a genuine engine.
*(Revenue is fuel for the mission, never the mission. It is weighted last on purpose.)*

### The Cost — subtracted ×2

**Difficulty** — capital, time, operational load, risk.
`1` trivial · `3` a real project · `5` a company-defining undertaking.
*(Difficulty is a brake. The harder something is, the higher the bar it must clear on the Cathedral axes to be worth it.)*

### The formula

> **Priority = (3 × Emotional) + (3 × Timelessness) + (3 × Alignment) + (2 × Defensibility) + (2 × Brand) + (1 × Revenue) − (2 × Difficulty)**

Maximum ≈ **68**. This math is intentional: the soul of the temple counts three times as much as its income, and difficulty actively pulls priority down. If a revenue-rich idea scores low here, that is the framework working — protecting the temple from becoming a startup chasing features.

## Gate 3 — The Timing (which year?)

Score tells you *how good*. This tells you *when*. Place an idea in a year band using these questions:

- **Is the current One Thing done?** If not, almost everything else is Year-later by definition.
- **Are its preconditions met?** (Listed per idea in Future Temple.) Unmet preconditions push the band out.
- **How high is Difficulty / capital?** High difficulty belongs later, when the temple can support it.
- **Does the digital body need to be complete first?** Most physical things do.

**Rough bands:**
- **Year 1** — deepens the *proven digital core* and the free→sacred funnel; low difficulty; no unmet preconditions.
- **Year 2** — extends the world and the "carry-home" objects; the core is beloved.
- **Year 3** — first physical expressions and the app; the digital body is complete.
- **Year 5** — larger physical works via partners; the brand is iconic.
- **Year 10** — institutional and legacy; a body of work exists that time has made undeniable.

> A high score does **not** mean "build now." The book scores near the top and still belongs to Year 2–3, because its preconditions (mature mythology, enough artwork) aren't met yet. Score = worthiness. Band = readiness. Both must be true.
